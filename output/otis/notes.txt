--- 10-K | Period ending 2025-12-31 ---

  [FX forwards / hedging policy]
    - To manage certain of those exposures, we use derivative instruments, including forward contracts.

  [FX exposure]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk Our primary market exposures are to fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices and interest rates.
    - To quantify our market risk exposure, we perform a sensitivity analysis based on hypothetical changes in foreign currency exchange rates and interest rates .

  [Interest rate risk]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk Our primary market exposures are to fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, commodity prices and interest rates.
    - To quantify our market risk exposure, we perform a sensitivity analysis based on hypothetical changes in foreign currency exchange rates and interest rates .

  [Counterparty / credit]
    - We diversify the counterparties used and monitor the concentration of risk to limit our counterparty exposure.
