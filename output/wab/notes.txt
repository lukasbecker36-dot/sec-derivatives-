  [FX exposure]
    - Foreign Currency Exchange Rate Risk The Company is exposed to certain risks associated with changes in foreign currency exchange rates to the extent our operations are conducted in currencies other than the U.S. dollar.
    - To reduce the impact of changes in currency exchange rates, the Company has periodically entered into foreign currency forward contracts.

  [Interest rate risk]
    - Interest Rate Risk In the ordinary course of business, Wabtec is exposed to risks that increases in interest rates may adversely affect funding costs associated with its available variable-rate debt facilities or new debt issuances.
    - At December 31, 2024, the Company's interest rate risk related to variable-rate debt was limited to the amounts borrowed and outstanding under the Delayed Draw Term Loan and the Term Loan.
