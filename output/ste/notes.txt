  [FX exposure]
    - QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK In the ordinary course of business, we are exposed to various risks, including, but not limited to, interest rate, foreign currency, and commodity risks.
    - For additional information regarding our debt structure, refer to Note 8 to our consolidated financial statements titled, “Debt.” FOREIGN CURRENCY RISK We are exposed to the impact of foreign currency exchange fluctuations.
    - This foreign currency exchange risk arises when we conduct business in a currency other than the U.S. dollar.
    - The financial statements of subsidiaries are translated to their U.S. dollar equivalents at end-of-period exchange rates for assets and liabilities and at average currency exchange rates for revenues and expenses.
    - Transaction gains and losses arising from fluctuations in currency exchange rates on transactions denominated in currencies other than the functional currency are recognized in the Consolidated Statements of Income.
    - Since we operate internationally and approximately 27% of our revenues and 26% of our Cost of revenues are generated outside the United States, foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations can significantly impact our financial position, results of operations, and competitive position.
