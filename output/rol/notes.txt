  [Interest rate risk]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures about Market Risk Market Risk The Company is subject to interest rate risk exposure through borrowings on its $1.0 billion revolving credit facility (the "Revolving Credit Facility") and on its commercial paper program.
