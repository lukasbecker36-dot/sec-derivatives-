--- 10-K | Period ending 2025-12-31 ---

  [FX forwards / hedging policy]
    - To ensure the adequacy and effectiveness of our foreign exchange hedge positions, we continually monitor our foreign exchange forward positions.
    - However, given the inherent limitations of forecasting and the anticipatory nature of exposures intended to be hedged, we cannot provide any assurances that such programs will offset more than a portion of the adverse financial impact resulting from unfavorable movements in foreign exchange rates.
    - We enter into forward foreign currency contracts that economically hedge the gains and losses generated by the re-measurement of certain recorded assets and liabilities in a non-functional currency.

  [FX exposure]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk We are exposed to financial market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates.
    - However, given the inherent limitations of forecasting and the anticipatory nature of exposures intended to be hedged, we cannot provide any assurances that such programs will offset more than a portion of the adverse financial impact resulting from unfavorable movements in foreign exchange rates.
    - Due to the materiality of our transactions in these local currencies, our results are impacted by changes in currency exchange rates measured against the U.S. dollar.

  [Debt / notes issuance]
    - We have no interest rate exposure to rate changes on our fixed rate debt, which totaled $3,004.9 million.

  [Interest rate risk]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk We are exposed to financial market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency exchange rates.
    - We have no interest rate exposure to rate changes on our fixed rate debt, which totaled $3,004.9 million.
    - We would have interest rate exposure with respect to our Revolving Credit Facility, however, there were no borrowings outstanding on the Revolving Credit Facility as of December 31, 2025.
    - If we had not repaid the outstanding balance on the Revolving Credit Facility on December 31, 2025, we estimate a 50-basis point increase in interest rates would have impacted our expected annual interest expense for the next 12 months by approximately $1.9 million.

  [Valuation]
    - Changes in the fair value of these undesignated hedges are recognized in other income and expense immediately as an offset to the changes in the fair value of the assets or liabilities being hedged.
