  [Counterparty / credit]
    - Counterparties to Linde’s derivatives are major banking institutions with credit ratings of investment grade or better.
    - The company has Credit Support Annexes ("CSAs") in place for certain entities with their principal counterparties to minimize potential default risk and to mitigate counterparty risk.
    - Management believes the risk of incurring losses on derivative contracts related to credit risk is remote and any losses would be immaterial.
    - These contracts are entered into primarily with major banking institutions thereby minimizing the risk of credit loss.
