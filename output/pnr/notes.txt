--- 10-K | Period ending 2025-12-31 ---

  [Interest rate risk]
    - We are exposed to various market risks, including changes in interest rates and foreign currency rates.
    - Periodically, we use derivative financial instruments to manage or reduce the impact of changes in interest rates and foreign currency rates.

  [Counterparty / credit]
    - Counterparties to all derivative contracts are major financial institutions.

  [Valuation]
    - QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK Market risk is the potential economic loss that may result from adverse changes in the fair value of financial instruments.
