--- 10-Q | Period ending 2026-01-31 ---

  [FX forwards / hedging policy]
    - We do not hedge our exposure to translation risks arising from fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates.

  [FX exposure]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk Our principal exposures to financial market risk are interest rate risk and foreign currency exchange rate and translation risk.
    - Our international operations are subject to risks associated with foreign exchange rate volatility, which could have a material and adverse impact on our future results of operations.
    - Fluctuations in foreign currencies also create volatility in our consolidated financial position because we are required to remeasure substantially all assets and liabilities held by our foreign subsidiaries at the current exchange rate at the close of the accounting period.
    - At July 31, 2025, the cumulative effect of foreign exchange rate fluctuations on our consolidated financial position was a net translation loss of $120.3 million.
    - We do not hedge our exposure to translation risks arising from fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates.

  [Interest rate risk]
    - Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk Our principal exposures to financial market risk are interest rate risk and foreign currency exchange rate and translation risk.
    - As the interest rates on a material portion of our cash and cash equivalents are variable, a change in interest rates earned on our investment portfolio would impact interest income along with cash flows, but would not materially impact the fair market value of the related underlying instruments.
    - If interest rates were to increase by 10% it would not materially affect our operating results.

  [Cash / liquidity]
    - Treasury Bills, and money market funds as of July 31, 2025.
