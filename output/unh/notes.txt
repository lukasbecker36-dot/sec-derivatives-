--- 10-K | Period ending 2025-12-31 ---

  [Debt / notes issuance]
    - QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK Our primary market risks are exposures to changes in interest rates impacting our investment income and interest expense and the fair value of certain of our fixed-rate investments, including our loan receivables, and debt.
    - The fair value of our fixed-rate investments and debt also varies with market interest rates.
    - As of December 31, 2025, $48 billion of our investments were fixed-rate debt securities and $51 billion of our debt was non-swapped fixed-rate term debt.
    - An increase in market interest rates decreases the market value of fixed-rate investments and fixed-rate debt.
    - Conversely, a decrease in market interest rates increases the market value of fixed-rate investments and fixed-rate debt.
