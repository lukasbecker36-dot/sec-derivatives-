    - Changes in exchange rates with respect to amounts recorded in our consolidated balance sheets related to these items will result in unrealized foreign currency transaction gains and losses based upon period-end exchange rates.
    - We also record realized foreign currency transaction gains and losses upon settlement of the transactions.
    - Moreover, we will experience fluctuations in our revenues and expenses solely as a result of changes in foreign currency exchange rates.
    - We may enter into derivative instruments that change in value as foreign currency exchange rates change to hedge certain exposures associated with affiliate revenue, the cost of producing or acquiring content, certain intercompany transactions, or in connection with forecasted business combinations.
