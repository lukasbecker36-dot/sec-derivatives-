  [Interest rate risk]
    - In January 2020, we entered into an interest rate swap agreement that is designated as a cash flow hedge for accounting purposes to effectively convert a portion of our floating-rate debt to a fixed-rate basis.
    - The swap agreement expires in October 2026, has a notional amount of $ 500.0 million and ensures that a portion of our floating-rate debt does not exceed 3.445 %.
    - The principal objective of this contract is to reduce the variability of the cash flow in our variable rate interest payments associated with our senior secured term loan B facility, thus reducing the impact of interest rate changes on future interest expense.
    - Cash flows associated with the interest rate swap agreement are reflected as cash flows from operating activities within our consolidated statements of cash flows.
