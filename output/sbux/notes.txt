  [FX forwards / hedging policy]
    - Forward contracts and commodity futures contracts are agreements to buy or sell a quantity of a currency or commodity at a predetermined future date and at a predetermined rate or price.
    - Excluding interest rate hedging instruments and cross-currency swaps, we generally do not enter into derivative instruments with maturities longer than three years.
    - Derivatives Not Designated As Hedging Instruments We also enter into certain foreign currency forward contracts, commodity futures contracts, collars, and swaps that are not designated as hedging instruments for accounting purposes.

  [Interest rate risk]
    - Excluding interest rate hedging instruments and cross-currency swaps, we generally do not enter into derivative instruments with maturities longer than three years.

  [Counterparty / credit]
    - We are allowed to net settle transactions with respective counterparties for certain derivative contracts, inclusive of interest rate swaps and foreign currency forwards, with a single, net amount payable by one party to the other.
    - By using these derivative instruments, we expose ourselves to potential credit risk.
    - Credit risk is the failure of the counterparty to perform under the terms of the derivative contract.
    - We minimize this credit risk by entering into transactions with carefully selected, credit-worthy counterparties and distribute contracts among several financial institutions to reduce the concentration of credit risk.
    - Receivables, net of Allowance for Credit Losses Our receivables are mainly generated from product and equipment sales to, and royalties from, our licensees, as well as from our Global Coffee Alliance and other Channel Development customers.
    - Our credit loss exposure is mainly concentrated in our accounts receivable portfolio.
    - Our allowance for credit losses is calculated using a loss-rate method based on historical experience, current market conditions, and reasonable forecasts.
    - For the fiscal year ended September 28, 2025 , we did not observe a significant deterioration of our receivable portfolio that required a significant increase in our allowance for credit losses.
    - As of September 28, 2025, and September 29, 2024, our allowance for credit losses was $ 24.0 million a nd $ 21.2 million, respectively.
