    - A financial instrument’s classification within the fair value hierarchy is based upon the lowest level of input that is significant to the fair value measurement.
    - Three levels of inputs may be used to measure fair value: Level 1 — Observable inputs that reflect quoted prices (unadjusted) for identical assets or liabilities in active markets.
    - Level 2 — Other inputs that are directly or indirectly observable in the marketplace.
    - Level 3 — Unobservable inputs that are supported by little or no market activity.
