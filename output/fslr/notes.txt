    - Interest Rate Risk Variable Rate Debt Exposure.
    - We are exposed to interest rate risk as certain of our debt arrangements have variable interest rates, exposing us to variability in interest expense and cash flows.

    - Accordingly, a change in prevailing interest rates may cause the market value of such investments to fluctuate.
