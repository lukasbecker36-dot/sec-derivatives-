    - The principal objectives of our investment management activities are to preserve capital while earning net investment income that is commensurate with acceptable levels of interest rate, default and liquidity risk, taking into account our funding needs.
    - As part of our investment management strategy, we may use derivative financial products such as interest rate hedges and interest rate swaps.
