  [Counterparty / credit]
    - Additionally, we are exposed to counterparty credit losses in the event of non-performance or non-payment.
    - We have a credit management process, which is used to assess, monitor and mitigate counterparty exposure.
    - In the event of non-performance or non-payment by a major counterparty, there may be a material adverse impact on our financial condition, results of operations or net cash flows.
    - To reduce price risk caused by market fluctuations, we enter into supply contracts and derivative contracts, including forwards, futures, swaps, and options with approved counterparties.
