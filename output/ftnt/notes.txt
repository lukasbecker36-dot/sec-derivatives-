    - To help protect against significant fluctuations in value and the volatility of future cash flows caused by changes in currency exchange rates, we engage in foreign currency risk management activities to minimize the impact of balance sheet items denominated in CAD.
    - We recognized an expense of $4.9 million in 2025 due to foreign currency transaction losses.
    - Our use of forward exchange contracts is intended to reduce, but not eliminate, the impact of currency exchange rate movements.
