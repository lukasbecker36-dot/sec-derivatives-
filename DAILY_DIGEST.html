Leads

Rockwell Automation (ROK) — 10-K, fiscal year ended 2026-09-30, filed 2026-11-12. The filing discloses de-designation/re-designation activity on its cross-currency swap book used to hedge net investments in foreign subsidiaries: "At the effective date of the amendments, the existing cross-currency swaps were de-designated from previous hedge accounting, and then designated as an after-tax net investment hedge of certain CHF and CNY functional currency denominated subsidiaries," with the swaps' notional rising to $850 million at September 30, 2026 from $800 million a year earlier. Its non-designated FX notional fell to $531 million from $799 million at September 30, 2025 (-33.5%), while the company's own stated 10% FX sensitivity widened to a $53 million pre-tax hit from $35 million the prior year — in its words, "a 10 percent adverse change in the underlying foreign currency exchange rates would reduce our pre-tax income by approximately $53 million."
Starbucks (SBUX) — 10-K, fiscal year ended 2026-09-27, filed 2026-11-12. The new 10-K restates hedge-accounting policy without reporting any numeric derivatives data this cycle: it notes that if a cash-flow-hedged transaction becomes improbable, de-designated derivatives "may be accounted for prospectively as non-designated derivatives until maturity, re-designated to new hedging relationships, or terminated early," and separately reaffirms that "transactions related to our designated cash flow hedges are probable to occur." No notional, fair-value or sensitivity figures were extracted from this filing — the entry carries policy language only.
Tyson Foods (TSN) — 10-K, fiscal year ended 2026-10-03, filed 2026-11-16. The filing puts fixed-rate debt at $7,701 million carrying a 5.1% weighted-average rate: "At October 3, 2026, we had fixed-rate debt of $7,701 million with a weighted average interest rate of 5.1%."
