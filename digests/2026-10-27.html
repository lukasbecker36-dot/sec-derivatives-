Leads

AZO — AutoZone's fiscal 2026 10-K (year ended August 29, 2026, filed October 26, 2026) shows growing Mexican-peso FX exposure: net assets in its Mexican subsidiaries ended the year at $1.3 billion, up from $893.1 million a year earlier, as "the year-end exchange rates with respect to the Mexican peso increased by 9.0% and 5.9% with respect to the U.S. dollar during fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2025, respectively." AutoZone's own disclosure puts a number on the resulting sensitivity: "The potential loss in value of our net assets in the Mexican subsidiaries resulting from a hypothetical 10 percent adverse change in quoted foreign currency exchange rates at August 29, 2026, and August 30, 2025, would have been approximately $116.6 million and approximately $81.2 million, respectively." The filing also flags a hedge-accounting policy nuance: "However, to the extent that the change in value of an interest rate hedge instrument does not perfectly offset the change in the value of the cash flow being hedged, that ineffective portion is immediately recognized in earnings."
