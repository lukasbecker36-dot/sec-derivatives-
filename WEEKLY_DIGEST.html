Story Leads

Cognizant doubles FX hedges, kills non-designated book. CTSH designated FX notional jumped from $85M to $174M (+105%) in Q1 2026 while non-designated positions collapsed from $71M to just $3M. This is a textbook shift toward hedge accounting treatment — smoothing P&L volatility by qualifying hedges for AOCI deferral. With Cognizant growing its offshore delivery model, the move suggests treasury expects sustained FX exposure and wants it off the income statement.

DoorDash unwinds $3.7B Deliveroo FX forward. DASH put on a deal-contingent GBP forward worth £2.8B (~$3.7B) in mid-2025 for the Deliveroo acquisition. By Q1 2026 the forward is gone — either the deal settled and the hedge did its job, or the transaction fell through. At the same time, non-marketable equity investments nearly tripled to $123M and the filing notes new GBP exposure. Clean M&A derivatives case study either way.

Ford reshuffles $164B derivatives book: less FX, more rates. F cut FX cash flow hedge notional 28% from $22.7B to $16.4B, while IR fair value hedges jumped 16% to $26.5B. Total notional grew modestly to $163.6B. Ford Credit appears to be rotating out of FX protection and into rate positioning. New sensitivity disclosure puts a 10% currency move at $3B and a 100bp rate shift at $66M — meaningful numbers for a company this leveraged.

Hilton's $1.6B interest rate swap matures — not replaced. HLT IR swap notional dropped from $1.6B to zero when the position matured in March 2026. The filing confirms this was expected, but Hilton hasn't put on a replacement hedge. With $3.1B in variable-rate debt on the books, the company is now fully exposed to rate movements — a bet that rates are heading lower?

Boeing commodity hedges expire, FX book swings underwater. BA non-designated commodity notional fell from $129M to zero as aluminium hedges expired without renewal. FX OCI swung from a +$210M gain to a −$25M loss — a $235M reversal in Q1. Derivative liabilities rose 44% to $127M. Boeing's filing says "no significant changes" to market risk, which sits oddly against the numbers.

Apple's debt portfolio marks down hard. AAPL gross unrealised gains on debt securities fell 70% from $280M to $83M in Q1 2026, tracking the rate backup. The $101B marketable securities portfolio is under pressure. Not a derivatives story strictly, but important context for the largest corporate treasury in the S&P 500.
