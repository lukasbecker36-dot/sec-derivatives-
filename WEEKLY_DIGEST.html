12–19 June 2026
A quieter week for new activations, but the pipeline processed several fresh Q2 filings with genuine signals — Smucker's is rapidly unwinding its commodity hedge book, Adobe's full $9B derivatives program is visible for the first time, and Dell's $22B hedge book just entered the tracking universe.

Story Leads

Smucker's slashes commodity hedges by two-thirds. SJM commodity derivatives notional fell from $1.28B to $403M in the January quarter — the third consecutive decline from a $1.87B peak last July. Net derivative position flipped from −$48M to +$5M, and derivative liabilities collapsed from $62M to $6M. This looks like a deliberate, sustained unwind of commodity protection. Worth asking: is Smucker's taking a view that input costs have peaked, or restructuring its hedge program post-Hostess integration?

Adobe's $9B hedging program finally comes into focus. ADBE Q2 10-Q (period ending May 29) revealed $6.16B in designated FX forwards and $2.7B in interest rate swaps. Prior filings showed placeholder-level numbers ($27M FX, $12M IR) that the LLM flagged as clearly extracted from the wrong section. This is the real Adobe derivatives book. The filing also flags $26M in expected AOCI reclassification losses over the next 12 months, suggesting the hedges are currently out of the money.

Dell's $22B hedge book enters the universe. DELL first derivatives capture from the May 10-Q: $15.3B in FX derivatives ($8.6B designated, $6.7B non-designated), $6.8B in IR swaps, and a net derivative liability of $19M. One of the larger corporate hedging programs in the S&P 500 — now trackable quarter over quarter.

Broadcom's post-VMware debt mountain: $67B fixed-rate. AVGO first interest rate data: $66.7B in fixed-rate debt principal, fair-valued at $62.5B. A 100bp rate move would shift that fair value by $3.8B. No derivatives extracted yet, but the sheer scale of the debt stack — and the $4.2B below-par discount — is the story for any journalist covering tech leverage.

Brown-Forman ramps FX forwards, discloses agave and barley exposure. BF.B 10-K (April 2026) showed $586M in FX forwards, up 27% from $463M the prior year. Commodity exposures span corn, malted barley, agave, rye, natural gas, and aluminium. Useful baseline as tariff-driven FX volatility tests whether consumer staples companies are hedging more aggressively.

NetApp pulls back on FX hedges. NTAP FX derivatives notional dropped 25% to $1.17B in January, from $1.65B last July. The cut is concentrated in non-designated hedges ($1.53B → $1.04B), while cash flow hedges held steady at ~$127M. May signal reduced non-USD revenue expectations or a hedge strategy shift.
