Story Leads

Abbott triples IR swaps to $4.2B as Exact Sciences deal takes shape. Abbott Laboratories’ Q1 2026 filing shows interest rate swap notional jumping from $1.2B to $4.2B (+250%), with the pipeline flagging additional hedges “associated with the Exact Sciences acquisition.” Simultaneously, FX derivatives contracted 42% to $7.6B and $13.4B in non-designated FX hedges dropped to zero. Classic M&A hedging pattern — locking in financing costs while unwinding legacy currency positions.

Dow Chemical’s derivatives book flips to net positive. Dow’s net derivative position swung from −$367M to +$93M in Q1, driven by IR swaps nearly tripling ($697M → $2.03B) and derivative liabilities plunging 71%. FX notional also grew to $18.5B. The chemical giant appears to have repositioned aggressively for the rate environment — worth asking if they’re locking in ahead of expected cuts.

Caterpillar ramps hedging as positions move against it. CAT quintupled its FX derivatives ($62M → $293M) and tripled commodity hedges ($10M → $37M) in Q1. But cash flow hedge AOCI flipped from a +$163M gain to a −$67M loss, suggesting existing hedges are underwater. Industrial bellwether expanding protection while its current book shows stress.

Micron’s FX hedge programme hits $22B. This week’s fresh Q3 extraction shows Micron’s total FX notional at $22.1B, with non-designated hedges up 79% to $12.4B. The memory chipmaker is running one of the largest corporate FX programmes in the S&P 500. Commodity notional appeared to drop 90%, but the pipeline flagged the prior-period figure as a likely data error.

AbbVie doubles IR swaps while $19.6B FX forward book vanishes. IR notional jumped from $1.8B to $4.08B (+127%) in Q1. The $19.6B FX forwards outstanding dropped to zero, though 7 of 16 tracked fields also disappeared — likely a partial extraction. The IR expansion looks real; the FX unwind needs manual verification against the filing.

Salesforce quietly builds a $3.5B private equity portfolio. CRM’s equity sensitivity to private holdings grew from $79M to $292M over nine months as top-5 private investments expanded from $1.3B to $3.5B. Meanwhile, public equity holdings were almost entirely exited ($68M → $5M). Unusual concentration of private-market risk for a SaaS company.
