Story Leads

Abbott triples interest-rate swaps for Exact Sciences deal. ABT IR swap notional jumped from $1.2B to $4.2B (+250%) in Q1 while total FX derivatives notional dropped 41.5% to $7.6B. Non-designated FX positions went to zero from $13.4B. The LLM extraction flagged the IR expansion as tied to "additional interest rate hedge contracts associated with the Exact Sciences acquisition." A company locking in $3B of new rate hedges for M&A financing is worth a call to their treasury team.

GM's hedge AOCI swings $391M in a single quarter. GM cash-flow hedge accumulated OCI collapsed from $385M to negative $6M — essentially a complete wipeout. Derivative liabilities dropped 29% to $836M and FX notional grew 14% to $8.2B. That AOCI reversal suggests a large tranche of hedges settled and were reclassified into earnings. The Q1 10-Q should have the breakout of what rolled off — with $39B in IR swaps and $8.2B in FX on the book, the remaining hedge portfolio is still substantial.

Constellation Energy's derivative book balloons to $13.2B in liabilities. CEG IR swap notional more than tripled from ~$1.4B to $4.6B in Q1. Total derivative liabilities hit $13.2B, posted collateral surged from zero to $1.7B, and the fair-value maturity profile more than doubled to $2.5B. For a nuclear-heavy generator, the scale of energy derivatives activity — and the collateral demands that come with it — is a story about commodity risk management at a critical moment for the power sector.

Ecolab halves FX hedge notional. ECL designated FX notional dropped 49.5% from $4.3B to $2.2B between its 10-K and Q1 10-Q. The IR swap book held steady at $1.5B. Whether this reflects maturities rolling off or a deliberate pullback from hedging international revenues is worth checking against their earnings call.

Hormel steadily building commodity hedge book. HRL commodity hedge fair value has nearly tripled over three quarters: $7M → $15M → $20M. Meanwhile, foreign currency net position dropped 27% from $1.1B to $800M, with the Philippine peso replacing Brazil's real in their major currency exposures. A food company increasing commodity protection while shifting EM FX exposure tells a story about changing supply-chain geography.
