Story Leads

HP Inc.’s derivative book flips from a net liability to a net asset. Comparing the fiscal Q2 10-Q (period ended April 30, 2026) to the fiscal Q3 10-Q (period ended July 31, 2026), HPQ’s total derivative asset rose from $163M to $210M (+28.8%), while total derivative liability fell from $252M to $169M (−32.9%). Together those moves swung the company’s net derivative position from an $89M net liability to a $41M net asset — a $130M turnaround. The expected 12-month AOCI reclassification also flipped, from −$79M to $21M. No filing text explaining either move is captured in notes.txt for this period (see Worth Asking).

Salesforce’s FX hedging notional and rate sensitivity contract sharply. Comparing the fiscal Q1 FY2027 10-Q (period ended April 30, 2026) to the fiscal Q2 FY2027 10-Q (period ended July 31, 2026), CRM’s total FX derivatives notional fell from $19,200M to $14,600M (−24.0%), with the non-designated portion falling from $17,800M to $13,200M (−25.8%). The 100bp interest-rate sensitivity figure fell from $50M to $36M (−28.0%). This quarter’s filing also introduced a new field, 10% equity price sensitivity, populated for the first time at −$641M — there is no prior value to compare it against. No filing text explaining the FX notional decline is captured in notes.txt (see Worth Asking).

Workday’s derivative liability narrows sharply; asset position grows. Comparing the fiscal Q1 FY2027 10-Q (period ended April 30, 2026) to the fiscal Q2 FY2027 10-Q (period ended July 31, 2026), WDAY’s total derivative asset rose from $27M to $44M (+63.0%), while total derivative liability fell from $128M to $94M (−26.6%). The net derivative position — still a net liability — narrowed from −$101M to −$50M (+50.5%). The 100bp interest-rate sensitivity figure also fell, from $54M to $43M (−20.4%). No filing text explaining these moves is captured in notes.txt (see Worth Asking).

Ulta Beauty’s credit-facility borrowings more than double. Comparing the fiscal Q1 FY2027 10-Q (period ended May 2, 2026) to the fiscal Q2 FY2027 10-Q (period ended August 1, 2026), ULTA’s outstanding borrowings rose from $144.9M to $339.6M (+134.4%). alert_log.txt notes this figure is directly stated in the filing text rather than an extraction artifact. This is a credit-facility draw rather than a derivatives position, but it bears on the company’s variable-rate interest exposure (see Worth Asking).
