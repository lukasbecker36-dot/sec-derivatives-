Story Leads

Ford's $2.7B derivative loss spiral is over — but questions remain. Ford's total derivative liabilities exploded 380% mid-2025 (from $558M to $2.67B) as FX headwinds hammered its international business. Collateral posted collapsed 74%. By March 2026, the portfolio has normalised — net position near zero on $163B notional. The hidden story: did Ford's credit deterioration in 2025 force tougher collateral terms from counterparties? Collateral held fell from $38M to $4M.
Boeing exits commodity hedging entirely. Commodity non-designated derivatives dropped to zero in Q1 2026, down from $129M. Boeing simultaneously cut FX designated notional 28% (from $22.7B to $16.4B) and derivative assets fell 57%. With MAX production restarting and 787 ramping up, the FX reduction is counterintuitive — this looks like cash preservation rather than risk confidence. Worth asking: is Boeing hedging less because it can't afford the margin calls?
Merck builds interest rate defences with $750M in new swaps. IR swap notional jumped 43% to $2.5B after Merck added two $250M forward-starting swaps in February. Expected 12-month AOCI reclassification collapsed 61% (from $178M to $69M), suggesting either better hedge performance or a revised rate outlook. Pharma companies don't typically make aggressive IR bets — Merck is positioning for something.
Meta's derivatives disclosures vanished in Q1 2026. 13 of 17 previously-populated derivative fields disappeared from Meta's latest 10-Q — FX forwards, IR sensitivity, equity hedges, all gone. This from a company that just disclosed $59B in fixed-rate notes and $20B in private equity positions. Either an extraction failure or a material disclosure change worth flagging to the SEC beat.
GE Vernova reveals full hedging programme post-spin. First complete quarterly derivatives disclosure shows $54B notional across FX ($49B) and commodity instruments. Derivative liabilities of $1.18B are normal for the portfolio size. This is the most detailed look yet at what GE's energy business was hedging before separation — and how the standalone company is rebuilding its risk management.
