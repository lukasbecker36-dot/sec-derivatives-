SEC Derivatives Weekly

31 July – 14 August 2026 (Q2 2026 earnings season)

Q2 filings dominate this batch: Molson Coors closed out a decade-old rate swap the day it issued new bonds, Akamai nearly doubled its convertible debt stack, and several large-cap FX hedgers — Apple, Merck, Pfizer — trimmed their notional books simultaneously. Below are the moves we can verify end to end, plus a longer-than-usual extraction-gap list from this quarter’s filing wave.

Story Leads

Molson Coors unwinds its $1B rate swap for a $107.5M gain, timed to a new bond deal. TAP’s interest-rate swap notional fell from $1,000M (Mar 31, 2026) to $0 (Jun 30, 2026). The 10-Q explains why: “In conjunction with issuing our 2036 USD senior notes, we settled our forward starting interest rate swaps for a realized gain of $107.5 million which was recorded to AOCI and will be amortized as a benefit to interest expense over the term of the 2036 USD senior notes.” The swap dated to a 2018 forward-starting position with a July 2026 termination date — it simply matured into the refinancing.

Akamai’s convertible debt jumps 85% to $7.64B. AKAM’s convertible notes principal rose from $4,140M (Mar 31, 2026) to $7,640M (Jun 30, 2026). The 10-Q confirms the year-end figure directly: “As of June 30, 2026, we had $7,640.0 million in aggregate principal amount of convertible senior notes outstanding.” That’s a new issuance roughly on top of the existing book, not a refinancing of it.

Republic Services cuts floating-rate debt by more than a third. RSG’s variable-rate debt outstanding fell from $2,800M (Mar 31, 2026) to $1,800M (Jun 30, 2026). Confirmed verbatim in the 10-Q: “As of June 30, 2026, we had $1.8 billion of principal floating rate debt.”

P&G’s FX-designated notional up 38% to $13.6B. PG’s FX-designated notional rose from $9,860M (quarter-end Mar 31, 2026) to $13,587M (fiscal year-end Jun 30, 2026 — P&G’s FY2026 10-K; note this spans a quarter-to-year-end gap, not a clean quarter-on-quarter read). The FY2026 10-K attributes the increase in net-investment-hedge notional to timing: “The increase in notional balance of the derivative instruments designated as net investment hedges is primarily driven by the Company’s decision to leverage favorable interest rate spreads in the foreign currency swap market.” Separately, total derivative liability flipped from −$568M to +$401M over the same span — the filing excerpt doesn’t say why (see Worth Asking).

Apple’s FX derivatives book shrinks by a fifth. AAPL’s total FX derivatives notional fell from $154,304M (Mar 28, 2026) to $125,366M (Jun 27, 2026), −18.8%. Almost all of that came from the non-designated side: $96,341M → $64,053M, −33.5%, while the designated book actually grew slightly, $57,963M → $61,313M, +5.8%. Over the same quarter, cash equivalents fair value fell $45,572M → $39,544M (−13.2%) and marketable securities fair value rose $101,023M → $106,973M (+5.9%). No cause given in the filing excerpt for the notional swing.

Merck and Pfizer both trim FX hedge books the same quarter. MRK’s FX forward notional fell from $42,106M (Mar 31, 2026) to $34,226M (Jun 30, 2026), −18.7%, concentrated in the not-designated portion ($25,789M → $21,373M, −17.1%). PFE’s not-designated FX notional fell from $17,134M (Mar 29, 2026) to $15,709M (Jun 28, 2026), −8.3%, while its interest-rate swap notional rose from $7,995M to $9,245M (+15.6%). Two separate issuers, two separate filings — no shared cause is stated in either, and we’re not asserting one.

Amazon: debt-securities fair value down 31%, equity marks up sharply. AMZN’s corporate debt securities fair value fell from $86,495M (Mar 31, 2026) to $59,431M (Jun 30, 2026), a $27,064M drop. Over the same period, marketable equity value rose from $3,800M to $7,400M (+94.7%) and marketable equity securities fair value from $2,801M to $4,687M (+67.3%). The 10-Q separately states “our equity and equity warrant investments in publicly traded companies represent $7.4 billion of our investments as of June 30, 2026,” consistent with the marketable-equity-value figure above.

Worth Asking

  • P&G: what flipped total derivative liability from a $568M liability to a $401M asset position in one quarter?
  • Apple: why did non-designated FX notional fall a third while the designated book grew — a shift in hedge program mix, or something else?
  • Merck / Pfizer: neither filing excerpt gives a reason for this quarter’s FX notional pullback.
  • Disney: net derivative position fell from −$55M (Mar 28, 2026) to −$779M (Jun 27, 2026); the filing excerpt doesn’t explain the swing.
