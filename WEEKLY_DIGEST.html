Story Leads

Adobe’s $6.2B FX hedge book revealed. The Q3 FY2026 10-Q (period ending May 29, processed June 16) shows $6.16B in designated FX derivatives and $2.7B in interest rate swaps. Prior extractions badly undercounted these at ~$27M due to section-capture issues — the LLM flagged that "$6.16B is clearly stated in the filing." This is the first clean read of Adobe’s real hedging programme, and it’s substantial. Worth checking whether the ramp reflects the Figma integration or broader international revenue growth.

AbbVie more than doubles IR swap positions. IR swap notional jumped from $1.8B to $4.08B (+127%) in Q1 2026, while total derivative assets rose 70% to $155M. FX derivatives held steady at ~$9.3B. With roughly $60B in long-term debt post-Allergan/ImmunoMedics, AbbVie appears to be aggressively re-layering its rate hedges — possibly locking in ahead of expected Fed moves.

Abbott cuts FX derivatives programme by 42%. FX derivatives notional dropped from $13B to $7.6B quarter-over-quarter. The $1.2B IR swap position also disappeared from the latest filing. Abbott generates ~60% of revenue internationally, so a pullback of this size in FX hedging is notable. Could signal a strategic shift or portfolio restructuring post-diagnostics spinoff discussions.

Targa Resources massively expands commodity hedging. Commodity derivative liabilities surged from $67M to $481M, with $650M in new notional. Variable-rate borrowings grew from $161M to $1.06B. The midstream operator looks to be layering on significant commodity risk management as it takes on leverage — a story angle on energy hedging discipline.

Molson Coors doubles commodity hedge book. Commodity derivatives notional went from $442M to $889M (+101%) in Q1 2026. The brewer is likely expanding hedges on aluminium, barley, and natural gas. Total derivative liabilities collapsed from $4.8B to $7M, though the prior figure looks like an extraction artefact.

Airbnb quietly enters the IR swap market. A $1.7B interest rate swap position appeared for the first time in Q1 2026, alongside its modest ~$26M FX programme. Airbnb’s balance sheet is mostly cash-rich, so a swap of this size likely relates to its $2B term loan — worth a call to their treasury team.

Brown-Forman boosts FX forwards 27%. The annual 10-K (April 2026) shows $586M in FX forwards, up from $463M. The Jack Daniel’s parent hedges euro, sterling, and AUD, and also discloses commodity exposures across agave, rye, corn, malted barley, and aluminium. A tidy package for a commodities-and-FX hedging story.
