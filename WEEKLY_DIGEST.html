SEC Derivatives Weekly

21–28 August 2026

Seven filings across seven issuers this week: Parker Hannifin, Sysco, Home Depot, Bio-Techne, Nvidia, J.M. Smucker and Synopsys. Three produced clean, verifiable moves — Parker Hannifin’s derivative book shrank on both sides, J.M. Smucker’s derivative liability roughly halved while its commodity sensitivity narrowed sharply, and Nvidia’s non-designated FX hedge notional grew by a fifth. Sysco’s FY2026 10-K produced this issuer’s first-ever populated row, so there is no prior period to compare it against. Bio-Techne’s 10-K and Synopsys’s 10-Q came back mostly blank, and Home Depot and Nvidia each lost several previously-populated fields even where other numbers held up — see Extraction gaps.

Story Leads

Parker Hannifin’s derivative book shrinks on both sides in its FY2026 10-K. Comparing the fiscal Q3 10-Q (period ended March 31, 2026) to the FY2026 10-K (period ended June 30, 2026), PH’s total derivative liability rose from $14M to $22M (+57.1%), while its net derivative position — still a net asset — fell from $16M to $7M (−56.2%). The 100bp interest-rate sensitivity figure fell from $21M to $10M (−52.4%) over the same period. No filing text explaining these moves is captured in notes.txt for this period (see Worth Asking).

J.M. Smucker’s derivative liability roughly halves; commodity sensitivity narrows sharply. Comparing the FY2026 10-K (period ended April 30, 2026) to the fiscal Q1 FY2027 10-Q (period ended July 31, 2026), SJM’s total derivative liability fell from $11.4M to $5.9M (−48.2%), and its net derivative position — a net asset — grew from $20.8M to $25.6M (+23.1%). Commodity price sensitivity narrowed on both ends of its disclosed range: the high scenario fell from $43.6M to $28.1M (−35.6%) and the average scenario from $7.0M to $2.2M (−68.6%). The underlying commodity derivatives notional also declined, from $606.2M to $562.0M, consistent with the tighter band. No filing text explaining the driver is captured in notes.txt (see Worth Asking).

Nvidia’s non-designated FX hedge notional grows 21.7%; new equity-derivatives line appears. Comparing the fiscal Q1 FY2027 10-Q (period ended April 26, 2026) to the fiscal Q2 FY2027 10-Q (period ended July 26, 2026), NVDA’s non-designated FX hedge notional rose from $1,850M to $2,252M (+21.7%). This quarter’s filing also introduced a new field, equity derivatives notional, populated for the first time at $5,800M — there is no prior value to compare it against. Five previously-populated fair-value-note fields (cash equivalents, marketable securities, corporate debt securities, marketable equity value and its 10% sensitivity) went blank this quarter; see Extraction gaps.

Sysco: first successful extraction, notable exclusions flagged. SYY’s FY2026 10-K (period ended June 27, 2026) is the first period this issuer has produced a populated row in tracking.csv, so no period-over-period comparison is possible. The filing shows an interest-rate swap notional of $2,300M, a total derivative liability of $30M, a net derivative position of −$18M, and a 10% equity sensitivity of $259M — the latter applied to the U.S. retirement plan’s broader investment portfolio, not narrowly to marketable equity securities, a scope note worth carrying into any story. alert_log.txt flags that the $2,300M swap figure excludes $2 billion of forward-starting interest-rate swaps on anticipated FY2027 debt issuance and $6.3 billion of deal-contingent interest-rate lock agreements tied to the pending JRD acquisition financing — both distinct instrument types not captured in this total (see Worth Asking).
