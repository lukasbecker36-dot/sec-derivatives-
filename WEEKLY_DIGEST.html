Story Leads

Marathon Petroleum's commodity derivatives blow up in Q1. MPC reported $390M in realized losses and $519M in unrealized losses on commodity derivative positions in Q1 2026 alone. For context, the prior nine months (through Sept 2025) showed cumulative realized losses of just $59M with a $22M unrealized gain. MPC doesn't designate these as hedges for accounting purposes. Worth asking: what drove the swing — crude price moves, position sizing, or a strategy shift?

DoorDash's $4.2B Deliveroo FX forward appears and vanishes. DASH put on a deal-contingent GBP forward worth roughly £2.8B (~$4.2B) in mid-2025 to hedge the Deliveroo acquisition. By Q1 2026 the forward has dropped back to zero — deal closed or unwound. Meanwhile, non-marketable equity investments nearly tripled from $47M to $123M. A clean case study of M&A-linked derivatives activity.

Edison International's derivative book collapses. EIX gross derivative assets fell from $196M (June 2025) to just $19M in Q1 2026 — a 90% drop. Congestion revenue rights notional plunged from 5,751 GWh to 1,232 GWh. Electricity notionals are fluctuating quarter-to-quarter. Is SCE significantly reducing its commodity hedging, or is this a seasonal pattern?

GoDaddy's IR swap program drives AOCI swing. GDDY went from zero derivative liabilities in Dec 2024 to $164M by June 2025. The position has since moderated to $103M, and AOCI on derivatives swung from -$39M to +$5.9M in Q1 2026. This looks like a new interest rate hedging programme that's now working in the company's favour.

DTE Energy eliminates floating-rate debt exposure. DTE took floating-rate debt from $597M (2.6% of total) in Q2 2025 down to $216M in Q3, and to zero in Q1 2026. At the same time, net credit exposure on energy trading rebounded from $1.08B to $1.65B. A deliberate de-risking on rates while expanding commodity trading counterparty exposure.

Autodesk restructures FX hedge mix. ADSK cut sold forward notionals 26% (from $2.17B to $1.60B) while growing option notionals 17% in both directions. USD depreciation sensitivity improved 37% to -$94M. Looks like a conscious shift from forwards to options — paying for more flexibility.
